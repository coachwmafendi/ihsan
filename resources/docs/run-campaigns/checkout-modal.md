# Checkout modal

The checkout modal lets donors complete a donation without leaving your website.

## How it works

- A donor clicks a donate button on your page.
- The modal opens with a pre-configured donation form.
- The donor selects an amount, fills in their details, and pays.
- The modal closes automatically after a successful payment.

## Configuration

You can set default amounts, suggested amounts, and recurring options from the element settings. The modal is accessible and keyboard-friendly.
