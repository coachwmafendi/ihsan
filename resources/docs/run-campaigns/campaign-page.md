# Campaign page

Every campaign has a public page that you can share with supporters.

![Public campaign page](/images/docs/campaign-public-page.png)

## What appears on the page

- Campaign title, description, and cover image.
- Fundraising progress bar and goal.
- Donation form or call-to-action button.
- Recent donor count and activity updates.

## Customization

You can customize the page color, button text, and layout from the campaign editor. The page is responsive and works on mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers.
