# Organizations and accounts

An Ihsan organization represents a nonprofit, project, or fundraising entity. Each organization has its own campaigns, donors, payment settings, and team members.

## Organization setup

- Enter your organization name, address, and contact details during onboarding.
- Upload a logo to brand the campaign pages and donor emails.
- Connect a payment provider to start accepting donations.

## User accounts

Multiple people can manage the same organization with their own user accounts. Invite team members from the team settings and assign roles as needed. Each user can belong to more than one organization.
