# Payment methods

Ihsan connects to leading payment providers so donors can pay using their preferred method.

![Payment settings](/images/docs/app-settings-payment.png)

## Supported providers

- **Stripe** — cards, wallets, and international payments.
- **CHIP** — popular in Malaysia for local bank transfers and FPX.
- **Cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and other major card networks.
- **FPX** — online bank transfer for Malaysian donors.

## Setup

Connect your Stripe account or enable CHIP from the **Payment** settings page. Test credentials can be used in test mode before switching to live payments.
