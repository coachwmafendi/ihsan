# Donor portal

The donor portal gives supporters a secure space to view and manage their giving history.

![Donor portal login](/images/docs/login.png)

## What donors can do

- View past donations and receipts.
- Update payment methods for recurring subscriptions.
- Download annual giving statements.
- Pause or cancel recurring donations.

## Access

Donors receive a magic link by email that signs them into the portal without needing a password. Admins can resend the link from the donor record.
