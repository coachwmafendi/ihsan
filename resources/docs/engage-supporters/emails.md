# Emails

Ihsan sends automatic emails to keep donors informed and your team up to date.

## Donor emails

- Donation receipt with a PDF attachment.
- Recurring subscription confirmation.
- Subscription cancellation notice.
- Donor portal access link.

## Admin notifications

Organization admins can choose which events trigger email notifications, such as new donations, failed payments, refunds, and daily summaries.

Manage notification preferences from **Settings > Notifications**.
