# Subscriptions

Subscriptions let supporters give automatically on a recurring schedule.

## Supported schedules

- Monthly recurring donations.
- Custom intervals configured during checkout.

## Managing subscriptions

Admins can view active, paused, and cancelled subscriptions from the **Supporters > Subscriptions** page. Donors can manage their own subscriptions from the donor portal.

When a payment fails, Ihsan records the failure and notifies the organization based on the notification settings.
