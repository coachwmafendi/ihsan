# Data export

Export your fundraising data for accounting, reporting, or external analysis.

![Donation export](/images/docs/app-donations-export.png)

## Supported formats

- CSV for spreadsheets and reporting tools.
- PDF for receipts and summary reports.

## What you can export

- Donations filtered by date range, campaign, or status.
- Supporter lists with contact information.
- Subscription and payout summaries.

Exports respect the filters you have applied, so you only download the data you need.
