# Supporters

Supporters are the people and organizations that have given to your campaigns.

![Supporters page](/images/docs/app-supporters.png)

## Supporter records

- Contact details and giving history.
- Total donated and average gift size.
- Active subscriptions and recent activity.
- Tags and notes for internal use.

You can export supporter data for use in your CRM or mailing list, respecting the privacy settings you have configured.
